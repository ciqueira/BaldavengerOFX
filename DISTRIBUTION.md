# Binary Distribution

Selected BaldavengerOFX binaries are independently built and distributed
through Nexus services and the MCNexus app. The software remains licensed under GNU GPLv3; the Nexus
distribution mechanism and optional support purchase do not add restrictions
to the GPL rights of recipients.

Every binary release should provide or clearly identify:

- the GNU GPLv3 license;
- the original project and author attribution;
- this fork's repository URL;
- the exact release tag or source commit;
- complete corresponding source and build scripts under [SOURCE.md](SOURCE.md);
- supported operating systems, hosts, and processing backends; and
- known material changes from upstream.

Recipients may copy, modify, and redistribute received GPLv3 software under the
license. Technical OpenKey records are used by Nexus to organize activation,
downloads, and updates; they do not replace the GPL license or prohibit a
recipient from exercising GPLv3 rights.

The optional Supporter purchase covers support and operational communications,
not ownership of the software or permission to use it.
